What do you see here?
  • Interactive charts panel: comparisons, box-plots, cut point trends, top improvements, size vs improvement, and heatmap.
  • Filter by iterations, cut points, and discretizers (depending on chart).
What each chart answers
  • Accuracy comparison: means by classifier.
  • Box-plot: dispersion by discretizer.
  • Trend by cuts: evolution 3p/4p/5p/up (Local/MDLP only).
  • Top improvements: where Local outperforms bases the most.
  • Size vs improvement: effect of samples (log and linear line).
  • Heatmap: who wins vs Local by discretizer and dataset.
How to use it
  • Choose the chart, apply filters, and enable/disable discretizers.
  • Download any visual as PNG from the side button.
Tips
  • Iterations=10it is the basis for the main conclusions.
  • In "trend-cuts" keep only Local/MDLP; in heatmap quickly observe where Local loses/wins.
